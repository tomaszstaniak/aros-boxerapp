Replacement icons in assets/source/replacements/ and assets/runtime/replacements/

These stand in for the Apple system images NSRevealFreestandingTemplate,
NSLockLockedTemplate and NSLockUnlockedTemplate (category b), plus the
full-screen toolbar glyph. Per-file sources, hashes, commands and UI use:
assets/replacement-assets.json.

History: until 2026-10-02 Tango Desktop Project 0.8.90 (public domain)
stood in for gamefolder.icns, import.png, prompt.icns and Game.png. Boxer's
originals are used again (assets/NOTICE-boxer.txt). The Tango sources remain
in the source tree only, unused (LICENSES/Tango-Public-Domain.txt).
